Novak Djokovic

Novak "Nole" Djokovic, from Belgrade, Serbia, holds the men's record of 24 Grand Slam singles titles, including 10 Australian Open titles. He has also spent more weeks ranked world No. 1 than any other player.

His return of serve and his flexibility let him turn defense into offense from almost anywhere on the court, on every surface.

Signature match

The 2012 Australian Open final against Rafael Nadal. It lasted 5 hours and 53 minutes, the longest Grand Slam final in history, and both players were too tired to stand during the trophy ceremony.
