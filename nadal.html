Rafael Nadal

Rafael "Rafa" Nadal, from Mallorca, Spain, is known as the King of Clay. He won 22 Grand Slam singles titles, including a record 14 French Open titles, before retiring in November 2024.

His heavy topspin forehand, left-handed, and his refusal to give up on any point made him the hardest opponent in the sport on a clay court.

Signature match

The 2008 Wimbledon final. Nadal beat Roger Federer 9–7 in the fifth set as darkness fell over Centre Court, ending Federer's run of five straight Wimbledon titles. Many fans still call it the greatest match ever played.
