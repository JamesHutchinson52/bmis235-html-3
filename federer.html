Roger Federer

Roger Federer, from Basel, Switzerland, won 20 Grand Slam singles titles, including a record 8 at Wimbledon. He held the world No. 1 ranking for a record 237 weeks in a row.

Fans called him "the Maestro" for his smooth one-handed backhand and a style that made hard shots look easy. He retired at the 2022 Laver Cup, playing his last match in doubles alongside Rafael Nadal.

Signature match

The 2017 Australian Open final. Coming back from six months off with a knee injury, Federer beat Nadal in five sets to win his first Grand Slam title in almost five years.
